SILVA, Andressa Santos; FRANÇA, Jean Pablo de Souza de; ALBUQUERQUE, Rafael Soares de; MELO, Wellington Júnior Torres de. **DARWIN: plataforma de previsão de demanda para supermercados regionais**. {{FOLHAS}} f. Trabalho (Disciplina Empreendedorismo) – Curso Superior de Tecnologia em Ciência de Dados, Faculdade de Tecnologia de Jundiaí "Deputado Ary Fossen", Centro Paula Souza, Jundiaí, 2026.

This work presents the business plan of Darwin, a data science company that offers, as software as a service (SaaS), a demand forecasting and inventory replenishment platform for supermarket chains with 5 to 50 stores in the State of São Paulo, Brazil. The problem addressed is replenishment based on fixed rules of the enterprise resource planning system and on the buyer's experience, which contributes to losses of 1.65% of retail revenue and out-of-stock rates above 10%. The objective is to demonstrate the market, technical, operational and financial feasibility of the company. The methodology combined documentary research in official sources, industry associations, market research firms, legislation and academic literature with explicitly stated estimates. The results indicate a serviceable market of about 350 chains and a target of 20 chains within 24 months; a product based on explainable gradient boosting, priced per store and with measurable targets for forecast error, out-of-stocks and losses; cloud operations costing less than 2% of revenue, with automated quality controls; and distribution through consultative selling, partners and customer success management. The study concludes that there is a market gap that can be served by an affordable solution, integrated with the existing system and proven through pilots, requiring about BRL 350 thousand in cash until break-even.

Keywords: entrepreneurship; data science; demand forecasting; supermarket retail; software as a service.
